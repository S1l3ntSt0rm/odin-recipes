chicken alfredo

Chicken Alfredo is a classic Italian-American dish that consists of tender pieces of chicken served in a rich and creamy Alfredo sauce. The sauce is typically made with butter, heavy cream, and Parmesan cheese, which gives it a velvety texture and a deliciously cheesy flavor. The chicken is usually cooked until it is juicy and flavorful, and then combined with the Alfredo sauce to create a comforting and satisfying meal. Chicken Alfredo is often served over fettuccine pasta, which helps to soak up the delicious sauce. It is a popular dish that can be enjoyed for lunch or dinner, and is often garnished with fresh parsley or additional Parmesan cheese for added flavor.

Prep time: 5 inutes

Cook time: 15 nutes

Total time: 20 nutes

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound uncooked fettuccine or linguine pasta
  • 2 boneless chicken breast halves, cooked and cubed
  • 1 (16 ounce) jar Alfredo-style pasta sauce
  • 1 (10 ounce) package frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 (4.5 ounce) can sliced mushrooms
  • ⅓ cup milk

Directions

  1. Fill a large pot with lightly salted water and bring to a rolling boil. Cook fettuccine at a boil until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes. Drain well.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, place cubed cooked chicken, Alfredo sauce, frozen vegetables, mushrooms, and milk in a large saucepan over medium-low heat. Cook and stir until chicken is heated through and vegetables are tender.
  3. Serve warm Alfredo and chicken sauce over cooked noodles.
